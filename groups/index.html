    Random Group Generator

    Split your class into random teams for group work, discussions or projects. Choose how many groups you want and press one button. The generator uses the same saved class list as the student picker, so there is nothing to paste again. Groups are dealt evenly, one student at a time, and shown large enough for the whole room.

    How to use it

    1. Save your class list once if you have not already. It is shared with the picker and the timer.
    2. Set the number of groups, from 2 to 12, then press Make groups or the spacebar.
    3. Not happy with the mix? Press again for a fresh shuffle, or press F to show the groups full screen.

    Frequently asked questions

    How does the team generator split uneven classes?

    Names are shuffled and dealt round-robin into the groups, so sizes differ by at most one student. With 26 students and 4 groups you get two groups of 7 and two of 6.

    Can I make pairs instead of groups?

    Yes. Set the number of groups to half your class size, rounded up. For a class of 28 choose 14 groups and every group is a pair.

    Can I fix certain students together or apart?

    Not yet. The generator is purely random, which is often the fairest option in front of a class. Re-shuffle until the mix works for you.
