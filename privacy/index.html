Privacy

Your class list never leaves this device. That is the design, not a promise: there is no server that could receive it.

What is stored, and where

The names you paste, your accent colour, theme and timer settings are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you are using. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data removes them. Another device or another browser starts empty.

What is not collected

  • No account, email address or password. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No student names are uploaded, logged or transmitted.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies are set by this site.
  • No third-party scripts or fonts are loaded. Everything is served from this domain.

Hosting logs

Like any website, the host that serves these files may record standard, anonymous request information such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, for security and traffic counting. This information is never combined with a class list, because the class list never reaches the host.

Children

Classroom Picker is a tool for teachers. It does not ask children for any information and is not directed at them. The names a teacher types stay under that teacher's control on their own device.
